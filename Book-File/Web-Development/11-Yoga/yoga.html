Welcome to Downward Dog Yoga

We host two awesome yoga spots in San Francisco and Berkeley, each filled with good vibes and diverse, welcoming communities. Whether you're a beginner looking for some guidance or are slightly more experienced, there's a mat here for everyone!

Explore a range of classes, from lively sessions to more relaxed ones, all designed to boost your flexibility and strength, while having fun at the same time. Our studios are decked out with everything you need, creating a space where you can truly unwind and elevate your practice.

At Downward Dog Yoga, it's not just about the poses – it's about building a community of wellness seekers like you. So, join us in San Francisco or Berkeley and let's make this journey a whole lot more fun!
