Metro Zoo Legal Information

Privacy Rights

The Metro Zoo is committed to protecting and respecting the privacy of its online users.

The Metro Zoological Society, a private 501(c)(3) organization, operates and maintains the Metro Zoo website (the “Site”). The purpose of this privacy policy is to give you confidence as you visit and use the site and to demonstrate our commitment to fair information practices and the protection of privacy. Your use of this website constitutes your acceptance of this privacy policy.

Information Collected

The Metro Zoo collects certain information that personally identifies you only when you wish to access certain services and information. The Site automatically recognizes visitors’ domain names and IP addresses (the number assigned to computers on the internet.) No personal information about you is revealed in this process. The Site may also gather anonymous “traffic data” that does not personally identify you for marketing purposes or for improving the services we offer.

Disclosure of Information

The Metro Zoo will not disclose or share any personal information you provide with any other party without your permission, except if required to do so in accordance with a court order, subpoena, or similar legal order, or state or local law, and in the event such disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, and safety of SFZS, its users, and other third parties.

The Metro Zoo does not sell its mailing list to any organization or company. From time to time, in an effort to increase the zoo's membership, exchanges or sells membership information – limited to names and addresses – with other selected institutions for a one-time use basis (for that institution). If you would like to opt out of these exchanges, please send us an email at membership@sfzoo.org with your name and membership information and we will remove your information from these lists.
