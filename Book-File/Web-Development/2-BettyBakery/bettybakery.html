Welcome to Betty's Bakery

Started in 2001 Betty's Bakery is a local organic bake shop founded by Betty Boop, a long time member of the community. Betty started her bakery in the back of her kitchen and has since expanded to serving pasteries over the entire state. We pride ourselves on our great handmade, fresh ingredient bake goods. Stop by and say 'hi'.

We serve:

  • Sourdough Bread
  • Rye Bread
  • French Baguettes
  • Croissants
  • Cupcakes
  • Donuts

Items with a yellow background are gluten free
